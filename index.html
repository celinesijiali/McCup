One day. Ridiculous challenges.

McCup is a series of silly competitions that bring McGill students together. Teams go head to head in a lineup of challenges, and anyone can join a team or just cheer from the sidelines. No skill required, and it's a good break from midterm stress.

Our goal is a memorable day and a stronger campus community. It's run by students in BUSA 303 as our class's catalytic event.

The Chill Zone

Grab food and drinks, watch the action, or hang out with friends. Proceeds go to charity or help us break even.

How it works

Teams of 4 to 6

Sign up ahead of time with a team name. Registration closes 2 weeks before the event.

Points on every challenge

Pick your challenges and see how many you can take on. A live leaderboard runs on a big screen all day.

Spirit points

Earn bonus points for costumes, team chants, and bringing the most spectators.

One McCup winner

The top team takes the cup at the end of the day.

FAQ

Who can play?

Anyone. No skill required. Teams are 4 to 6 people and sign up in advance with a team name.

When is the sign-up deadline?

Two weeks before the event, so around Nov 1. Final date to be confirmed.

Will there be a bonfire?

We're planning a closing bonfire, but it depends on getting a city permit.

Can I help run McCup next year?

Yes. Check the box on the sign-up form to join next year's organizing team.

Does it cost anything?

Details to be confirmed. Watching is meant to be free.

What if it rains?

The event moves indoors or adjusts to the weather. We'll post updates here and on social media.

Where exactly is it?

Lower Field at McGill, with an indoor or rink backup. We'll confirm the final location before Nov 15.

Where does Chill Zone money go?

To charity or toward breaking even on event costs.
